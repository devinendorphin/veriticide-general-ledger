# veriticide-general-ledger

**Register: formal / evidentiary.** The strictest register in the collection. Claims carry
epistemic status, boundaries are stated explicitly, and nothing is asserted past what the
evidence bears.

**Thesis:** documentation infrastructure for Track A of the Standing Protocol — automated
capture and protocol-grade analysis of institutional harm laundering. The deliverable is
`cases/`; `ledger/ledger.md` is the source-of-record archive they are cut from.

## Repo-specific discipline

- **Use this repo's own schemes.** Do not invent tagging. The five classifications
  (`SPECIMEN` · `CONTROL` · `NULL` · `SINCERE-UNBOUNDED` · `INSTRUMENT`), the six laundering
  moves, the tier taxonomy, and the evidence bands are defined in `docs/` — read them before
  writing an entry.
- **Every entry needs a BOUNDARY section** stating what the item establishes and what it
  does *not* establish alone. A single item is an instance; pattern is the proof.
- **Step one, never step two.** Case files assert a basis to demand preservation,
  disclosure, audit, and inquiry. A finding of guilt is for a tribunal.
- **Structural identity is not coordination.** The "one mechanism" claim is about a shared
  move-set, not collusion. Do not let drafting language blur that.
- **The Reflexivity Clause applies to you.** Analyst-as-subject discipline is not decorative;
  corpus-level deference is the failure mode this repo studies.
