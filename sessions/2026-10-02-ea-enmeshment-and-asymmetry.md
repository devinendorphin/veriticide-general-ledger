## Tensions and disagreements, both sides recorded

1. **The asymmetry is conceded, and the fix is only partly verified.** Claude conceded four
   asymmetries:
   - universal-form strawmen;
   - a documented-causal standard that discounts the private channels enmeshment uses;
   - adversarial checks that only subtract;
   - fragility headlined on a claim that passed its test.

   The audit then found that the fix document repeated the error: zeros scored on unexamined
   cells, and "no unit is a cult" resting on a silently exculpatory threshold reading. Both
   were corrected. The lesson: **declaring a fix is not applying one.** The audit was run by
   the party it audits, and only the ChatGPT adjudication can close that.

   contrary details felled hypotheses. In `ledger.md`, the institution's innocent reading
   almost never prevails, and the proposer judges each check. Claude's position: this is
   *not* proof of bias, because selection predicts a high fail rate. Blind re-adjudication is
   owed. Endorphin's position is not yet recorded. **Ask.**

   excluded because no decision-node tie was found. Under the later enmeshment lens, Endorphin
