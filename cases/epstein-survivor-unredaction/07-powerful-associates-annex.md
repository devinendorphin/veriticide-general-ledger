## Bearing on the charge: recorded in both directions (asymmetry-audit rule)

The charge's load-bearing leg B is "victim info revealed while **possible enablers concealed**."
That is a **tendency claim** about the *direction* of redaction.
- **This annex is a counter-instance to that leg.** A powerful associate's correspondence was
  *released, not concealed.* One counter-instance **lowers** the leg's weight. It does not
  defeat it: leg B rests on documented concealment of *other* possible enablers (CNN, P1).
- **Upgrade condition for leg B:** documented redaction of powerful associates' names in the
  same releases where survivors' names went unredacted, at a rate exceeding the redaction rate
  of survivors' information.
- **Downgrade condition for leg B:** powerful-associate records, Thiel's among them, turn out
  to be released at least as fully as survivor information was exposed. In that case the
  "inversion" reduces to a negligence failure, which is still Tier 3 harm but without the
  power-tracking direction.
- **Not assessed:** whether *other* parts of Thiel-related correspondence were redacted. The
  annex records only what the reporting says was released.
