# 03 — ADVERSARIAL CHECK

*This case lives or dies on its first defense. A framework that treats a state's objection to a
court's jurisdiction as a crime has become the thing it opposes. The check is written against the
packet first.*

---

## Defense 1 — "This is sovereign foreign policy. A non-party state is entitled to reject, oppose, and sanction a court that claims jurisdiction over its nationals without consent."

*The defining defense. If it is not answered cleanly, the case should not be brought.*

**Steel-man.** The United States never ratified the Rome Statute. Under the classic consent
principle of treaty law, a treaty does not bind non-parties. Congress codified hostility to ICC
jurisdiction over Americans in the American Servicemembers' Protection Act of 2002. Presidents of
both parties have rejected that jurisdiction. The Biden administration lifted the 2020 sanctions
but "left the official US opposition to the probe unchanged." Sanctions are a lawful foreign-policy
tool, authorized by IEEPA, and courts have not struck down E.O. 14203 on its face. Calling this
"veriticide" criminalizes a policy disagreement about a treaty and chills every state that objects
to an international court.

**Concession.** The jurisdictional position is the protected path. Rejecting the Court's
jurisdiction, refusing to cooperate, arguing that Art. 12(2)(a) cannot bind non-party nationals,
and urging allies to agree are all protected, however forceful. None of it is charged.
`00-charge-theory.md` removes it in the first row of the disconfirming test.

**What remains outside the concession.** Three acts are not argument about jurisdiction. They are
penalties on the people who adjudicate and testify:

- **Designating judges "for ruling to authorize."** The jurisdiction argument is available to the
  U.S. in many ways. It can make its case publicly, through an ally's Art. 19 challenge (Israel
  filed one and the Chamber left it open "without prejudice"), or by non-cooperation. Penalizing
  the judge for the ruling does not contest the ruling. It raises the cost of ruling.
- **Designating the rapporteur and the NGOs for testimony.** Their recommendations and
  submissions have, in a federal judge's words, "no binding effect." Penalizing them does not
  answer what they said. A neutral court reached the same reading on the Albanese record
  ("nothing more than speak").
- **Designating the institution with the aim of "crippling its ability to operate."** The
  operative protection in §8(d) of E.O. 14203 covers U.S. persons and allies' nationals. The
  disabling covers everyone: Darfur's victims awaiting reparations, the Philippines' "war on drugs"
  victims awaiting trial, and every other situation. A jurisdictional objection about Americans does
  not require the Court to stop working for Sudanese victims. The designation's reach exceeds the
  objection it rests on.

**The three-part wall for this case (adapted from the sibling packet).** The case is brought only
if all three hold:
(i) **the conduct targets adjudication or testimony, not only jurisdiction**;
(ii) **the actor holds power over the evidentiary field**: banking, travel, and IT "service" for
the Court, its staff, and its witnesses (Art. IV-bis(3)(a));
(iii) **characterization substitutes for engagement** (Art. IV-bis(3)(b)).
All three hold for forms 1–3. If a future designation targets only a jurisdictional act, or
engages the reasoning it opposes, it is outside this case.

## Defense 2 — "The Court is corrupt and politicized; its own Prosecutor was removed for serious misconduct."

**Steel-man.** The Assembly of States Parties found the Prosecutor committed "serious misconduct
and a serious breach of duty." The Sahel states, Venezuela, and Chad accuse the Court of selective
prosecution. "Corrupt and fatally politicized" is a fair description of an institution in that
state, and sanctioning it is a response to documented misconduct.

**Concession.** Khan's removal is real and adverse to the Court. It gives "corrupt" a partial
factual footing **as to one officer**. The selectivity critique of the Court is long-standing and
does not originate with this administration.

**Why it does not carry the designations.**
- The removal was done **by the Court's own oversight system** (82/125 states parties, after a
  UN OIOS investigation). That is evidence of a working internal accountability mechanism, which
  cuts against "unaccountable."
- **No designation names misconduct as its ground.** The stated grounds are rulings, votes,
  recommendations, and "efforts … to investigate." The judges designated "for ruling" are not
  accused of misconduct.
- The **institutional** designation (9 Oct) came **eleven weeks after** the Prosecutor's removal
  (24 Jul). The officer whose conduct grounds "corrupt" was already gone.
- **Residue:** a reader who holds that the selectivity critique alone justifies disabling the
  Court has a position. That position is a content argument, and it is protected. The designations
  do not make that argument; they make the jurisdictional one.

## Defense 3 — "It is staged, reversible, and diplomatic. Nothing has been crippled."

**Steel-man.** Four general licences keep the Court running for 180 days, keep IT and cloud
services, pay pensions, and protect detainees. The Secretary "hold[s] out hope for a diplomatic
solution." The High Commissioner himself said the sanctions "will not take effect immediately and
… service providers can continue to work." This is leverage, not destruction.

**Concession.** The staging is real counter-evidence on **immediacy and effect**. No proceeding is
shown to have been impaired by the 9 Oct designation. `04-falsification-memo.md` makes a
negotiated outcome that leaves judicial independence intact a falsifier of the effect claim.

**Why it does not reach the act.** The 180-day licence is "intended to provide time for States
Parties to demonstrate meaningful progress on … resolutions that address fundamental U.S.
concerns." The Court's continued operation is made **conditional on a non-party's satisfaction**,
and the stated concern is the Court's investigation of protected persons. The leverage is the
forum's function. The aim is stated without staging: "Either the ICC will end its threats, or we
will end the ICC." Staging the means does not withdraw the aim. Forms 1 and 2 (the designations of
persons) are not staged at all; they have been in effect since 2025.

## Defense 4 — "The instrument element fails: designations are discrete official acts, not a fluent justificatory instrument."

**Steel-man.** Art. II(3)(a) requires an instrument generating locally defensible justifications
at a scale beyond unaided human agency. A press statement and an OFAC listing are discrete acts.

**Why it fails, and why it stays contested.** The sibling packet's answer is that the office and
its megaphone are the instrument. This case adds a stronger mechanism (A1). An SDN listing is
executed **automatically** by every U.S. person's sanctions-screening system. Each bank refusal,
cancelled email account, and NGO that "stopped replying to emails from court officials" is locally
defensible as compliance. The aggregate is the forum's isolation, and it is produced at a speed and
scale no unaided agent commands. **Residue conceded:** a tribunal may hold that "instrument" means
a justification generator, not an enforcement network. If so, the case falls back on **Art. IV(3)**.
The characterization of the Court's and the rapporteur's pattern evidence as "baseless,"
"illegitimate," and "extreme and unfounded accusations" "may itself be considered an act under
Article II(2)(d)." Art. IV(3) does not require the instrument framing.

## Defense 5 — "The ICC is not the discernment commons. Victims still know what happened to them."

**Steel-man.** The Convention itself (Art. V(4)) says nothing confers on any external body the
authority to discern on behalf of an affected population. Disabling a court cannot "defeat
discernment" that resides in the population.

**Concession.** Agreed. The packet does not claim the affected populations' discernment is
defeated. The charge concerns the **formal, shared channel**: the place where a contested claim
is weighed under rules that both sides must accept. It also concerns the **penalties on those who
carry testimony into it**. That is the Tier 4 "correction environment." It is narrower than
"veriticide proper" might suggest, and the charge theory says so.

## Defense 6 — "The allies-only scope is just the limit of U.S. protective interest; calling it 'asymmetry' is rhetoric."

**Steel-man.** A state protects its own nationals and its allies'. That is not hypocrisy, and it is
not evidence of anything.

**Partial concession.** As policy, scoping protection to allies is unremarkable. The asymmetry
carries **moderate** weight and serves one purpose only. The stated principle ("officials whose
government has not consented") is general. The operative scope is allies only. In 2022 the Senate
unanimously supported the Court investigating non-party Russian nationals. The gap shows the
**content** reason given (non-consent) is not the operative criterion. That is a reason to read the
characterizations as standing attacks rather than content argument. Graded A1 and not
load-bearing.

## Defense 7 — "The characterizations of Albanese may be accurate."

**Steel-man.** Several governments and organizations have accused her of antisemitic statements.
If accurate, "unfit" is a fair description.

**Answer.** This packet does not adjudicate the characterizations. The discriminator is whether
the designation **engaged her claims**. It did not quote, cite, or rebut any of them, and its stated
ground is her recommendations. Whether or not the characterizations are accurate, they function in
the statement in place of an answer to her testimony. The neutral court's reading supports that
function.

## Defense 8 — "The analyst and the operator are not neutral."

**Steel-man.** The operator asked to "build a case." The analyst is a model whose developer is in
litigation with this administration. This packet is advocacy wearing a custody chain.

**Answer: conceded as a reason for review, not as a refutation.** Both conflicts are disclosed
below. The packet was **not** independently reviewed. Its first move was a disconfirming test that
removed four candidate charges. The custody store holds the respondent's own words, byte-fixed, so
a reader who distrusts the analysis can check the record without it.
