## The shape, read off the dates

1. **Persons before the institution.** For twenty months the designations target the people who
   rule (judges), prosecute (deputies), and testify (rapporteur; NGOs). The institution follows
   last.
2. **Effects public before escalation.** The AP effects report (May 2025) precedes every
   designation of persons after the Prosecutor. All three neutral court rulings (Jul 2025; May 2026;
   28 Sep 2026) precede the institutional designation.
3. **The adverse fact about the Court and the escalation are eleven weeks apart.** The Prosecutor's
   removal (24 Jul) precedes the institutional designation (9 Oct). The one officer whose conduct
   grounds "corrupt" was gone before the Court was designated.
