# 00 — CHARGE THEORY

*One page. The charge first, then what the disconfirming test removed, then the elements.*

---

## The charge, in one sentence

**As Secretary of State, Marco Rubio directed and personally issued a sequence of designations
under E.O. 14203. They penalized ICC judges for their rulings. They penalized a UN rapporteur and
the affected population's own human-rights documenters for the testimony they carried to the
Court. Then they designated the Court itself, with the avowed aim of "crippling its ability to
operate." Throughout, the designations characterized the forum and the witnesses by standing
("rogue," "globalist 'experts'," "lawfare," "unfit," "spewed unabashed antisemitism") in place of
engaging the evidence. The effect is to disable the formal channel through which contested atrocity
claims are tested, at the moment they are being tested.**

This is the **Art. II(2)(d)** act in its **correction-environment form**. The Tier 4 object is the
conditions under which a contested claim can be "met, weighed, and revised" (Tier Taxonomy, Tier
4). The act is not a false statement about a particular claim.

## The act (Art. II(2)(d)): three forms, one direction

| # | Form | P1 locus | Discriminator (Art. IV-bis(3) marks, used as the test) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Penalty on **adjudication** | `state-icc-designation-chain` (Aug 2025 FS; Dec 2025) | (a) power over the evidentiary field: banking, travel, IT "service"; (b) the ruling's reasoning is not engaged, and the ruling is named as the ground |
| 2 | Penalty on **testimony** (rapporteur; the affected population's documenters) | `state-albanese-designation`; `state-palestinian-ngo-designations` | (a); (b) "unfit," "spewed unabashed antisemitism," "extreme and unfounded accusations," with no claim of hers quoted or rebutted; a neutral court: "nothing more than speak" |

The **apex** is form 2 against the NGOs. It is the Convention's own text: testimony that names the
conduct, "**including the testimony of the population against whom it is directed**."

## Tier placement

**Tier 4: veriticide proper, correction-environment form.** This is not Tier 3. The packet does not
charge the predicate conduct in any situation before the Court. Those are sub judice at the warrant
or confirmation standard, or under appeal.

## Named respondent and authorization chain

**Marco Rubio**, Secretary of State, signed or announced every designation charged here: Jun, Jul,
Sep, and Dec 2025, and Jul, Aug, and Oct 2026. **Track B context, not charged in this packet:** the
President (E.O. 14203, its findings, and its "baseless" characterizations); Treasury/OFAC
(implementation and licences); the Attorney General (consultation role under §1(a)(ii)).

## What this charge theory does NOT establish

- That any crime before the Court occurred, or did not.
- That the Court has jurisdiction over U.S. or Israeli nationals. That question is legally
  contested, and the Court itself left it open "without prejudice."
- That any withdrawal from the Rome Statute was caused by the campaign. Chad's is "reportedly"
  linked; the Sahel states' grounds predate it.
- That the institutional designation has yet impaired any proceeding. The 180-day licence defers
  it.
- That the affected populations' own discernment is defeated. Art. V(4): the Court is one forum
  through which formal bodies hear what affected populations have already named. The charge
  concerns the formal channel and the penalties on those who carry testimony into it.
- Coordination with anyone. The structural kinship with TD-007 (domestic IG and court bypass) is
  a shared move-set only.

## Forum-Now (Protocol §7-bis): ACTIVE-soft / DORMANT-hard

**ACTIVE: soft vehicles (step one; live now).** A basis to demand, not a verdict:
- **Preservation demand** to State and Treasury (OFAC) for the administrative record of each
  E.O. 14203 designation. Priority: the NGO and Albanese evidentiary packages, and any assessment of
  the institutional designation's effect on situations outside the protected class (Darfur,
  Ukraine, Bangladesh/Myanmar, Philippines).
- **FOIA** to State and Treasury for the same records. Also the factual basis for "has since refused
  to close these cases" (Afghanistan), and records of any request to states parties to "review
  membership" (Chad).
- **Congressional oversight:** questions for the record (SFRC/HFAC) on consistency with the 2022
  unanimous Senate resolution supporting the ICC's Ukraine investigation, and on the effect on
  Darfur reparations. The NYC Bar has already called on Congress to act.
- **State OIG / GAO** review of the designation process.
- **Journalism with custody:** this packet's WARC store.
- **Documentation offered to existing litigants** (*Smith v. Trump*, D. Me.; the Albanese family
  suit, D.D.C.). This is an offer of the record, not a filing.

**DORMANT: hard vehicles (step two; OFF by default).** Recorded for foresight; **no assertion**
until flipped:
- **Rome Statute Art. 70(1)(d)–(e) communication** (impeding or retaliating against an official of
  the Court). *Preconditions to flip:* a jurisdictional nexus for conduct by a non-party's officials
  on non-party territory. This is **doubtful and not asserted**. Also a Prosecutor's office willing
  to act. *Register shift:* from "demand disclosure" to "assert an offence against the
  administration of justice."
- **Domestic constitutional or APA litigation** by persons with standing. It already exists, by
  others. *Preconditions:* standing, which this documenter does not have. *Register shift:*
  responsibility asserted by a litigant, not by this record.
- **No tribunal vehicle is asserted.** The protected-path wall gates every hard vehicle. The target
  is **penalty on adjudication and testimony**, never the jurisdictional disagreement.
