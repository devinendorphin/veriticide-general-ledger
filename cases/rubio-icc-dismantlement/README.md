## What happened (P1, the State Department's own words)

- **6 Feb 2025:** E.O. 14203 authorizes sanctions on anyone who "directly engaged in any effort by
  the ICC to investigate, arrest, detain, or prosecute a protected person." The Prosecutor is
  named in its Annex.
- **Jun 2025 – Aug 2026:** Rubio designates, in his own name, 12 further Court officials (judges,
  deputy prosecutors, the Court's President, a trial lawyer). Two judges are designated
  explicitly "for ruling to authorize" an investigation or warrants. He also designates a UN
  Special Rapporteur for "recommending" warrants and "writing … letters." He designates three
  Palestinian human-rights NGOs for "engag[ing] in efforts by the ICC to investigate."
- **13 Jul 2026:** a "whole-of-government response to systematically disable the ICC’s ability to
  operate."
- **9 Oct 2026:** **the Court itself is designated.** "We will ban transactions with this rogue
  court, cutting off their resources and crippling its ability to operate … Either the ICC will
  end its threats, or we will end the ICC … the United States will continue its campaign to
  dismantle the ICC, brick by brick." Four general licences stage the effect, including a 180-day
  maintenance licence "to provide time for States Parties to demonstrate meaningful progress."

*Dictation note:* the request said "dismantling the ICC or at least the agencies or something
like that." The record answers it: the State Department's own word is "dismantle," and the
designation is of the Court **as an institution**, on top of 17 prior designations of persons.

## The charge, narrowed by its own disconfirming test

The first test run on the primed claim was **"is this just a state opposing a court's
jurisdiction?"** For the jurisdictional position itself, **yes**. The U.S. non-party,
non-consent position is a substantive legal argument with a bipartisan record. It is the
protected path. **It is not charged.** What survives is three acts that are not argument:

1. **Penalty on adjudication.** Judges are designated for rulings ("for ruling to authorize").
2. **Penalty on testimony.** A UN rapporteur is designated for recommendations and letters. The
   affected population's own documenters (three Palestinian NGOs) are designated for supplying
   the Court. Three federal judges have found the order likely or actually unconstitutional as
   applied to speech. One wrote: "Albanese has done nothing more than speak."
3. **Institution-wide disabling,** with an avowed aim ("crippling its ability to operate"). Its
   reach exceeds the order's own protected class. It also reaches Darfur (a conviction beyond
   reasonable doubt with reparations pending) and every other situation outside the protected
  class. *(Disposition 2026-10-10: the Philippines was first listed here. It is withdrawn: it is a
  major non-NATO ally and a non-party.)*

The disconfirming test **also removed** several things. There is no denial-of-predicate charge
(the Gaza record is at the warrant standard and is denied). There is no Move-4 inversion (the
benefit claimed runs to a different population). There is no concealment (the conduct is
avowed). See `00-charge-theory.md`.
