## Source-quality note

- **"Sudan probe has ground to a halt"** rests on **one interested source**: counsel for an ICC
  prosecutor who is suing the administration. AP did not independently confirm it. It is graded S1
  with an interested-source flag. The Darfur trial that was already underway continued to a
  conviction (6 Oct 2025) and a sentence (9 Dec 2025); see `icc-non-ally-docket`. That fact limits
  how far "halted" can be read: it may concern the investigation, not the trial.
- **Microsoft cancellation:** "ICC staffers said"; AP reports Microsoft did not respond. S1,
  attributed.
- Anonymous ICC officials "out of fear of reprisal" are the source for several other claims.

## BOUNDARY

**Establishes:** that by May 2025, a major wire service had publicly reported the designations'
operational effects. Those reported effects were lost email, frozen accounts, NGOs ceasing
cooperation, staff departures, and a claimed halt in the Sudan investigation. The effect on
non-U.S./non-Israel situations was therefore on the public record 17 months before the
institutional designation.

**Does not establish alone:** that the reported effects are accurate in each instance (sources are
anonymous or interested). It does not establish which effects persisted, or that any atrocity went
unprosecuted because of them.
