## Press statement, Marco Rubio, September 4, 2025, verbatim (full body)

> Today, I am designating three foreign NGOs—Al Haq, Al Mezan Center for Human Rights (Al Mezan),
> and the Palestinian Centre for Human Rights (PCHR)—pursuant to Executive Order 14203, “Imposing
> Sanctions on the International Criminal Court.” These entities have directly engaged in efforts
> by the International Criminal Court (ICC) to investigate, arrest, detain, or prosecute Israeli
> nationals, without Israel’s consent.
>
> This administration has been clear: the United States and Israel are not party to the Rome
> Statute and are therefore not subject to the ICC’s authority. We oppose the ICC’s politicized
> agenda, overreach, and disregard for the sovereignty of the United States and that of our allies.
> The ongoing actions of the ICC set a dangerous precedent for all nations and we will actively
> oppose actions that threaten our national interests and infringe on the sovereignty of the United
> States and our allies, including Israel.
>
> The United States will continue to respond with significant and tangible consequences to protect
> our troops, our sovereignty, and our allies from the ICC’s disregard for sovereignty, and to
> punish entities that are complicit in its overreach.
>
> All targets are being designated pursuant to Section 1(a)(ii)(A) of Executive Order (E.O.) 14203.

## Analyst note (A1, separate from the record)

- The three designated entities are **Palestinian human-rights documentation organizations**. The
  stated ground is that they "directly engaged in efforts by the ICC to investigate." For an NGO
  that does not arrest or prosecute anyone, that can only mean supplying information or evidence.
  The State release does not say what the engagement was. *The NGOs' own descriptions of their ICC
  submissions are not captured this session (owed).*
- This fits Art. II(2)(d) of the Convention most directly: "testimony that names the conduct,
  **including the testimony of the population against whom it is directed**." The affected
  population's own documenters are penalized for supplying testimony to the forum.
- The release contains no characterization of the NGOs' findings, and no engagement with them
  either. The designation is the response.
- Al Jazeera (13 Jul 2026, `state-icc-campaign-launch`) describes the administration as having
  "imposed sanctions on ICC officials and rights groups that have provided evidence to the court."
  This is S1 corroboration of the evidence-provider reading.

## BOUNDARY

**Establishes:** that the Secretary of State designated three Palestinian human-rights NGOs for
"engag[ing] in efforts by the ICC to investigate," and that the stated response is to "punish
entities that are complicit."

**Does not establish alone:** what the NGOs submitted, whether it was accurate, or what effect the
designation had on their work. This session did not search for any U.S. court challenge by these
NGOs (U; owed).
