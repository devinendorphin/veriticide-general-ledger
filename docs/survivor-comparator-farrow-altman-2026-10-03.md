# Survivor comparator: Dylan Farrow ↔ Ann (Annie) Altman (2026-10-03)

*Operator-directed comparator for TD-009. Dylan Farrow is a similar case: a private person
who became public through her own testimony, about a powerful family member, given as an
adult about abuse she alleges she suffered as a child. Testimony comes first, per the standing
note. Allegations are recorded at summary level: what each woman has said publicly, as her
claim.*

**Provenance:** `[IN-FRAMEWORK]`, compiled by the proposer and not self-verified (Priority
32). **Grades:** court and prosecutorial records are P1/S1 via press relay; the survivors' own
statements are P2.

---

## 1. Dylan Farrow: her account first

- **The allegation.** She alleges that Woody Allen, her adoptive father, sexually assaulted her
  in August 1992 at the family's Connecticut home, when she was 7.
- **Her public testimony as an adult:**
  - an open letter, published through Nicholas Kristof's *New York Times* column (Feb 2014);
  - a first television interview (CBS, Jan 2018);
  - the HBO series *Allen v. Farrow* (Feb 2021).
- **Institutional record** (S1 relay):
  - **Connecticut prosecutor Frank Maco (Sept 1993):** said he had **"probable cause"** to
    prosecute, but declined to bring charges in order to spare the child a trial.
  - **Yale–New Haven Hospital clinic (1993):** concluded no abuse occurred. Its lead doctor's
    sworn statement theorized that Dylan was **"emotionally unstable"** and **coached** by her
    mother. Vanity Fair later reported that he had not personally interviewed Dylan, though
    the statement implied he had.
  - **Judge Elliott Wilk (NY custody case, 1993):** found **"no credible evidence"** that Mia
    Farrow coached Dylan, and called Allen "self-absorbed, untrustworthy and insensitive." He
    denied Allen visitation with Dylan. He found the abuse evidence **inconclusive**.
  - **Allen was never charged.** He has denied the allegation throughout.
- **The response:**
  - Allen's lawyer said the allegation was **"implanted"** by Mia Farrow (2014).
  - Moses Farrow, a sibling, published a 4,600-word essay in 2018 defending Allen and
    accusing Mia of abuse. Ronan Farrow, another sibling, supports Dylan.
  - **Institutional uptake:** Allen's memoir deal at Hachette (2020) was cancelled after an
    employee walkout. Another publisher released the memoir within weeks.

## 3. What the comparison does to TD-009's claims

- **"The reversed causal arrow is a recurring Move 3 form": upgraded from one instance to two.**
  In both cases the testifier's psychological state was used to disqualify her, without
  ruling out that the alleged abuse caused it. In Dylan's case a judge **rejected** the
  related coaching theory, so the disqualification there met an independent check and partly
  failed. Two instances are still not a pattern. A third independent case is needed.
  - *Upgrades if* further cases show distress treated as disqualifying while its alleged cause
    goes unexamined.
  - *Downgrades if* the disqualification turns out to rest, in these cases, on clinical
    evidence that rules out the alleged cause.
- **"The family dynamic is a very unique specimen" (operator): partly confirmed, partly
  narrowed.**
  - **Not unique:** the disqualification form. It recurs almost exactly in Farrow.
  - **Distinctive in Altman:** (1) **the whole family stands unanimously with the accused**, so
    there is no in-family corroborator; (2) **the testifier depends materially on the side
    that impeaches her**; (3) **the diamond**, care given in the giver's currency, set against
    a stated need for food. Farrow has none of the three. The comparison sharpens the
    operator's claim: what is unique is the **closure**, not the disqualification.
- **Farrow shows what Altman lacks: an independent check.** A prosecutor and a judge looked at
  the case from outside the family, and one rejected the accused side's central theory. That
  is the ledger's general thesis at household scale: harm tracks the absence of an
  *independent* check (comparison doc §4.1; H10).

## 4. Reflexivity note on the earlier block

This comparator **does not test** whether the earlier tool block favored the powerful. Both
accused here are powerful, so power is not varied. The discriminating test still needs a
comparable case with a **powerless** accused, run by a non-proposer. This file records both
allegations at the same summary level as TD-009. It does not route around the block.

## BOUNDARY

**Establishes:** the public record of both cases at the stated grades, and that the
disqualification form, pathologizing the testifier without examining the alleged cause,
appears in both.

**Does NOT establish:**
- the truth of either allegation (Allen was never charged; Altman is unadjudicated);
- coordination or influence between the two families (structural identity is not
  coordination);
- a base rate.
