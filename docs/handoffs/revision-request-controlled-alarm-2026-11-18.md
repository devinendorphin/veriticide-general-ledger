## Why this matters to your own thesis

Your video is about **controlled alarm**: alarm about an industry's product that the industry's
own community produces and contains. Exxon is the baseline case of controlled alarm.

- Its scientists' alarm was **accurate**. Their 1977–2003 projections matched later warming
  (Supran, Rahmstorf & Oreskes, *Science*, 2023).
- It was **kept inside**. In 1977–2014, 80% of Exxon's internal documents acknowledged that
  climate change is real and human-caused; 81% of its *NYT* advertorials expressed doubt
  (Supran & Oreskes, *ERL*, 2017).

Without that baseline, the viewer cannot see what is new in fromInside.ai. Nor can they ask the
question the Exxon case actually poses to a lab: **what does the institution hold internally that
its employees' interviews do not disclose?**

As cut, the video's accountability lands on one engineer's laugh. In the Exxon record, the debt
was the company's.
