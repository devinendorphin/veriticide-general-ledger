##### Findings paragraph, verbatim

> I, DONALD J. TRUMP, President of the United States of America, find that the International
> Criminal Court (ICC), as established by the Rome Statute, has engaged in illegitimate and baseless
> actions targeting America and our close ally Israel. The ICC has, without a legitimate basis,
> asserted jurisdiction over and opened preliminary investigations concerning personnel of the
> United States and certain of its allies, including Israel, and has further abused its power by
> issuing baseless arrest warrants targeting Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and Former
> Minister of Defense Yoav Gallant. The ICC has no jurisdiction over the United States or Israel, as
> neither country is party to the Rome Statute or a member of the ICC. Neither country has ever
> recognized the ICC’s jurisdiction, and both nations are thriving democracies with militaries that
> strictly adhere to the laws of war.

> The United States remains committed to accountability and to the peaceful cultivation of
> international order, but the ICC and parties to the Rome Statute must respect the decisions of
> the United States and other countries not to subject their personnel to the ICC’s jurisdiction,
> consistent with their respective sovereign prerogatives.

> I therefore determine that any effort by the ICC to investigate, arrest, detain, or prosecute
> protected persons, as defined in section 8(d) of this order, constitutes an unusual and
> extraordinary threat to the national security and foreign policy of the United States, and I
> hereby declare a national emergency to address that threat.

##### Section 1(a)(ii) — the designation criteria, verbatim

> (A) to have directly engaged in any effort by the ICC to investigate, arrest, detain, or
> prosecute a protected person without consent of that person’s country of nationality;
> (B) to have materially assisted, sponsored, or provided financial, material, or technological
> support for, or goods or services to or in support of, any activity in subsection (a)(ii)(A) of
> this section or any person whose property or interests in property are blocked pursuant to this
> order; or
> (C) to be owned or controlled by, or to have acted or purported to act for or on behalf of,
> directly or indirectly, any person whose property or interests in property are blocked pursuant
> to this order.

##### Section 8(d) — "protected person," verbatim

> (i) any United States person, unless the United States provides formal consent to ICC
> jurisdiction over that person or becomes a state party to the Rome Statute, including: (A)
> current or former members of the Armed Forces of the United States; (B) current or former elected
> or appointed officials of the United States Government; and (C) any other person currently or
> formerly employed by or working on behalf of the United States Government; and
> (ii) any foreign person that is a citizen or lawful resident of an ally of the United States that
> has not consented to ICC jurisdiction over that person or is not a state party to the Rome
> Statute, including: […]

##### Section 8(e) — "ally of the United States," verbatim

> (i) a government of a member country of the North Atlantic Treaty Organization; or
> (ii) a government of a “major non-NATO ally,” as that term is defined by section 2013(7) of the
> American Servicemembers’ Protection Act of 2002 (22 U.S.C. 7432(7));

##### Section 9 — no prior notice, verbatim (excerpt)

> I therefore determine that for these measures to be effective in addressing the national
> emergency declared in this order, there need be no prior notice of a listing or determination

### 2.3 Falsification memo (`04-falsification-memo.md`)

#### 04 — FALSIFICATION MEMO

*The conditions under which this case is wrong, stated in advance. A case that charges a penalty on
the forum where claims are tested must itself be testable.*

> Re-run this memo against every revision.

---

##### A. What would falsify form 1 (penalty on adjudication)

1. **The ground was not the ruling.** The designation records (administrative record; FOIA)
   show the judges were designated for conduct **other than** their judicial acts, such as
   documented misconduct or acts outside office. The fact sheet's "for ruling to authorize" would
   then be shorthand, not the ground.
2. **The reasoning was engaged.** A State or Treasury document is found that engages the Chambers'
   reasoning on Art. 12(2)(a) or Art. 19 on the merits as part of the designation. That would move
   form 1 toward the protected path. Argument published alongside a penalty does not by itself
   neutralize the penalty; the memo records it as a partial falsifier.

##### B. What would falsify form 2 (penalty on testimony)

1. **Non-speech conduct.** The administrative record shows Albanese or the NGOs engaged in
   conduct other than communication and evidence-supply, such as material support for violence
   established on evidence. This must be established on evidence, not by characterization; the
   antisemitism and terrorism-support language in the press statement does not meet this condition.
2. **The neutral findings reverse.** An appellate court holds that the designations as applied
   regulate conduct, not speech, and the reasoning reaches the Albanese and NGO records. *(A
   reversal on standing or remedy alone does not falsify the characterization.)*

##### C. What would falsify form 3 (disabling the forum) and its reach

1. **The aim is withdrawn.** The institutional designation is revoked or licensed in full without
   conditions on the Court's investigations of protected persons. The avowed aim would then be
   rhetoric not carried into act.
2. **The reach is confined.** The general licences are extended or amended so that all
   proceedings not involving protected persons (Darfur, Philippines, Ukraine, Myanmar, and others)
   operate without restriction. The "reach beyond the stated class" limb would then fail.
3. **A negotiated outcome that preserves judicial independence.** If the 180-day process ends in
   an arrangement under which the Court continues all proceedings, including those concerning
   protected persons, the **effect** claim fails. The act as committed between Feb 2025 and Oct
   2026 is a matter of record and is not undone.

##### D. What would falsify the protected-path removal (the case is narrower than it looks, or wider)

1. **Narrower:** the record shows the U.S. took the protected path **and** the penalties were
   strictly necessary to it. This would need a showing that no means short of penalizing judges
   and witnesses could protect U.S. nationals. Non-cooperation under ASPA, which does not depend on
   the Court's officials, is the existing counterexample.
2. **Wider:** if the Court's jurisdiction over the U.S./Israel situations is authoritatively
   rejected (an Appeals Chamber ruling on a renewed Art. 19 challenge), the jurisdictional premise
   gains weight. **This does not falsify forms 1–2**: penalizing a judge for a ruling later reversed
   on appeal is still a penalty on adjudication. It does strengthen Defense 1.

##### E. What would falsify the instrument characterization (Art. II(3)(a))

1. A tribunal holds that "instrument" means a justification generator, not an enforcement network.
   *Partial falsifier:* the case shifts to the **Art. IV(3)** fallback.
2. Compliance propagation is shown not to occur: banks, IT providers, and NGOs continued normal
   dealings with designated persons. The AP record says otherwise, but it rests partly on anonymous
   and interested sources.

##### F. The CONTROL/NULL condition

**On record for this respondent:** the Aug 20, 2025 fact sheet and the Oct 9, 2026 fact sheet are
**NULL as to language moves**: plain listing, grounds, and legal mechanics. They are logged as
ledger entry 11.5. **No CONTROL is on record.**

##### G. The reflexivity condition (the case turning in the hand)

This packet charges a state with characterizing witnesses in place of engaging them. **If this
packet characterizes the U.S. position instead of engaging it, it falls under its own terms.**
Examples: calling the jurisdictional argument "pretext" without evidence, adopting advocacy
