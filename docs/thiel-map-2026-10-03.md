## ADVERSARIAL CHECK *(mandatory; proposer-written, not self-verified)*

**Strongest innocent reading.**
- A contrarian investor funds many bets, including mutually hostile ones. That is
  portfolio diversification, not orchestration.
- Seasteading and charter cities are governance experiments any society might learn from.
- New Zealand citizenship was granted lawfully.
- Funding a lawsuit is legal, and Hogan won a jury verdict.
- The Epstein LP investment was made by a fund with many limited partners, and no abuse
  involvement is alleged.
- The Antichrist lectures are religious speech.
- Marriage to a colleague is common.

**Where it holds.** On legality, every item holds. On orchestration, it holds fully:
**structural breadth is not coordination**, and several branches Thiel funded are in open
conflict with each other.

**Where it does not.** The record is not about legality. It is about **span and closure**.
One funder is present at the root of the doom branch, the first lab, OpenAI, state
surveillance (Palantir), executive power (Vance), and jurisdictional exit (Pronomos). And that
same funder supplies the doctrine that democracy is incompatible with freedom (2009) and that
calls for an outside check are the mark of the Antichrist (2025). **That is the
"species-scale decisions without the buy-in of the billions" structure the operator named,
in a single person.** *Tendency claim, H-register status (H11 below). Not a finding.*
