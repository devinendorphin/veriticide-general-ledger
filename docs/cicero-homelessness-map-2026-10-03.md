## 2. The Cicero policy package (S1)

- **Origin.** The Cicero Institute was founded by **Joe Lonsdale, a Palantir co-founder** (with
  Peter Thiel). It is based in Texas.
- **Model bill (2021), copied into law in several states:**
  - public camping becomes a **misdemeanor**: in the model, up to a month in jail and a $5,000
    fine;
  - funding is redirected away from **Housing First** (housing without preconditions) toward
    **"treatment first"** (housing or aid conditioned on sobriety and treatment) and
    sanctioned encampments.
- **Missouri, the state where her family lives and where her lawsuit is:** a 2022 law built on
  the Cicero model made **sleeping on state land** punishable (up to 15 days in jail and a
  $500 fine after a warning) and **barred state funds from permanent housing**. The Missouri
  Supreme Court struck it down unanimously in **Dec 2023**, on single-subject grounds, not on
  the merits.
- **Federal:** Cicero filed an amicus brief for the city in ***Grants Pass v. Johnson***. The
  Supreme Court ruled in 2024 that camping bans may be enforced against people with nowhere
  else to go.
- **Executive Order 14321** (24 Jul 2025, "Ending Crime and Disorder on America's Streets"):
  - eases encampment removal;
  - expands **involuntary civil commitment**;
  - shifts federal money from Housing First to programs **requiring treatment and sobriety**,
    and to cities that enforce camping bans.

  Cicero, which had lobbied for many of these items, called it "a huge step."

## 5. Adversarial check *(proposer-adjudicated; pending a non-proposer, Priority 32)*

- **Strongest innocent reading.** Cicero's advocates argue that Housing First failed people
  with untreated addiction and mental illness, and that treatment first serves them better.
  That is a sincere policy position with public defenders. The network links are ordinary
  overlap in Silicon Valley capital, and do not show alignment.
- **Outcome:**
  - On **Cicero's sincerity, the innocent reading may prevail.** This file makes no finding on
    motive.
  - On **the policy's structure as it bears on her, the innocent reading fails as a defense of
    the structure.** Conditioning support on accepting treatment, backed by criminal penalty
    and commitment, removes the unconditional route whatever the intent. For a person whose
    credibility has been attacked through her mental-health status (TD-009), it also gives
    that same framing state power.
  - On **the brother-network link: weak, held as position only.** It would upgrade with
    evidence that Altman or OpenAI funded or endorsed Cicero or EO 14321. It would downgrade
    if OpenAI or Altman publicly opposed these policies.
