## H10 (hypothesis, LIVE)

**The checks against sexual power were themselves enmeshed.** See P-3 and P-5: the mechanisms
meant to protect complainants were run by people tied to the accused, or failed the
complainant. This extends the comparison doc's §4.1, where harm tracks the absence of an
*independent* check, to the protective mechanism itself.
- *Upgrades if* further cases show complaint-handling routed through people tied to the
  accused.
- *Downgrades if* independent handling, such as the outside law firm eventually used for P-2,
  becomes standard and later cases show it working.
